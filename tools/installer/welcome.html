Pressura

amplificator opticus: an optical-style compressor with a one-knob speed control and a drive mode.

This installs:

  • Audio Unit in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (Logic, GarageBand, Ableton…)
  • VST3 in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 (Ableton, Reaper, Bitwig, Cubase…)
  • Standalone app in /Applications

Click Customize on the Installation Type step to leave any of them out.

When it finishes, rescan plugins in your DAW. Pressura appears under gueibrielon.

Instala el plugin AU, el VST3 y la app Standalone. Al terminar, volvé a escanear los plugins en tu DAW.
