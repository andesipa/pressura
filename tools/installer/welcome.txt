Pressura - amplificator opticus
An optical-style compressor with a one-knob speed control and a drive mode.

This installs:
  - the VST3 plugin in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
    (Ableton, FL Studio, Reaper, Cubase, Bitwig...)
  - the Standalone app, with a Start menu shortcut

When it finishes, rescan plugins in your DAW. Pressura appears under "gueibrielon".

------------------------------------------------------------
Instala el plugin VST3 y la app Standalone. Al terminar, volve
a escanear los plugins en tu DAW.
